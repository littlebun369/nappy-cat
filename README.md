# nappy-mate
A quiet little sanctuary with five rooms, five cats and gentle thoughts.

*A little sanctuary for quiet moments.*

Nappy Mate is a small digital world with five little rooms, five cats and their own unusual alien companions.

Inspired by Japanese Zen interiors, gentle everyday moments and the ancient wisdom of the I Ching.

## The Five Rooms

- **Misty** — Quietness & Acceptance
- **Mochi** — Love & Connection
- **Susu** — Hope & New Beginnings
- **Pudding** — Letting Go & Rest
- **Coke** — Journeys & Possibilities

Each cat has its own little Q-version alien companion.

## The Garapon

A traditional Japanese-style wooden rotating lottery box brings together 64 Morandi-coloured glass marbles.

Each marble corresponds to one of the 64 I Ching hexagrams, offering a modern bilingual reflection in English and Traditional Chinese.

## 3 · 6 · 9 · Cycle

- **3** — The Foundation
- **6** — The Connection
- **9** — The Message
- **Cycle** — The Continuation

## Our Philosophy

You don't come here to find out what will happen.

You come here to find a little kindness for what is happening now.

No pressure. No tasks. Just a little world to visit and enjoy.
